panovi viewer is a browser-based viewer for zooming and panning around wide footage. No install, no sign-up. Paste a video URL and get close to whatever you want to see.

Try a sample video → Open the viewer
01 — How it works

Just three steps.

Paste a URL

Paste a YouTube URL, or a video file URL such as an mp4, into the field at the top.

Open

Press “Open video” and playback starts. Tick “Fullscreen” to open it edge to edge.

Zoom & move

Zoom with the wheel or a pinch, then drag to the spot you want. Double-click (double-tap) to return to the full view.

02 — What it opens

YouTube videos, and video files.

The URL type is detected automatically. Both zoom and pan the same way.

▶ YouTube

Public and unlisted videos work. These URL forms are supported.

  • https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…
  • https://youtu.be/…
  • https://www.youtube.com/shorts/…
  • An 11-character video ID

◼ Video files (mp4 etc.)

Any video file URL the browser can play directly. An mp4 (H.264) plays in almost every browser.

  • https://example.com/movie.mp4
  • A file URL on GitHub Pages, your own server, or cloud storage

For ultra-wide footage, publishing as an mp4 works best. With a video file the viewer reads the real aspect ratio, so “Fit” fills the screen height and you pan left and right from there. With YouTube, the player’s own black bars above and below get magnified along with the video.

About YouTube quality. The viewer cannot set YouTube’s quality for you. A prompt appears when the video opens: use the gear at the bottom right of the player → Quality → the highest resolution, then press “Done”. You can change it any time from “⚙ Quality” at the top right.

03 — Share as a link

Hand over the exact view, as a URL.

Add ?url=VIDEO_URL to the viewer’s address and the page loads that video as soon as it opens. When you open a video in the viewer, the address bar takes this form automatically. Use it as a bookmark, or share it by chat or email as is.

04 — Controls

Mouse, fingers, or keyboard.

The buttons under the video do the same jobs. “All” shows the whole frame, “Fit” fills the screen, and “Left / Center / Right” glide to that part of the frame.

05 — FAQ

Things you might wonder.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?
No. The viewer simply plays the video at the URL you enter, right in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. Only the zoom level and view position are kept inside your browser for the next time you open it.
A YouTube video will not open.
Videos whose uploader disallows playback on other sites, or with age, region or sign-in restrictions, cannot be opened here. Use the “Open on YouTube” link shown instead.
I entered an mp4 URL but it will not play.
The URL must be directly downloadable by the browser. Share-page URLs (such as Google Drive links) or URLs that require a login will not work. Some formats, like HEVC (H.265), do not play in every browser — an H.264 mp4 is the safe choice.
There is no sound when it opens.
Browsers often start muted because of autoplay rules. Press M to unmute.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. Pinch to zoom and move with one finger. Landscape gives you a bigger picture.
