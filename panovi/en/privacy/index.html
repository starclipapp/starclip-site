Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 — 日本語

panovi collects no personal information and no usage data. No account is required, and nothing is sent to external servers.

1. Information we collect

The iOS app “panovi” does not collect or transmit any user data — no personal information, no usage statistics, no identifiers, no location data. It contains no analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs.

2. Your footage

Recorded videos and stitched videos are stored only on your iPhone (inside the app’s own storage and your camera roll). No third party — including the developer — can access them.

Videos exported for YouTube (8K / 8K+VR) are also only saved on your device. The app never uploads anything automatically; whether and where to publish is entirely your own action.

3. Communication between devices

To link two iPhones (or three, when using one as a remote), the app uses the connections below. All of them are direct device-to-device connections and never pass through servers on the internet.

BluetoothUsed to quickly discover and pair with the other iPhone.
Local network / Wi-FiUsed to sync recording settings, start/stop recording together, and transfer video files.

4. Permissions and why they are needed

CameraRecording video.
MicrophoneRecording audio with the video.
Photo libraryFinding and stitching recorded videos, and saving stitched results.

5. In-app purchases

For stitches of 3 minutes or longer, the app offers two in-app purchases: an event pass (24 hours) and a one-time unlock. Purchases and payments are processed entirely by Apple (the App Store). The developer never receives payment details such as credit card numbers.

The record of your free stitches and event passes is stored only on your iPhone (in the keychain). It is never sent anywhere and contains nothing that identifies you.

6. Diagnostic logs

Diagnostic logs are stored only on the device. They are never sent anywhere unless you explicitly share them yourself.

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
