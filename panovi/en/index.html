01 — The whole scene, and your kid

The whole scene, or your kid?
You thought you could never keep both.

Filming a sports day or a recital is always a trade-off. Zoom out and your kid becomes a speck; zoom in and you lose the whole scene.

panovi removes that choice: shoot wide, zoom in when you watch. Each side records in 4K, so you keep far more detail than one camera covering the same width.

03 — Just set it down

You never have to aim the camera.

Once it sits on the tripod, you never touch it again. Because there is no aiming, none of this happens:

07 — Unlike a 360° camera

09 — How it works

There are only three steps.

01

Pair

Show a QR code on one iPhone, scan it with the other. That is it.

02

Place

Set the two side by side on the tripod. The screen shows the overlap while you adjust the angle.

03

Shoot, then stitch

Record and stitch. The finished video saves to your camera roll.

The second iPhone can be borrowed. Recording and transfer are free, so a family member’s or friend’s iPhone becomes your second camera the moment they install the free app. No shared Apple ID or iCloud needed — any two iPhones pair by reading a QR code (iOS 18 or later). The only thing that can ever cost money is a stitch of 3 minutes or longer, and only on your device doing the stitching — never on the borrowed one.

Lending or borrowing? Use Guided Access. Guided Access, built into every iPhone, keeps the borrowed iPhone locked to panovi. Nobody can go back to the Home Screen or open Photos or Messages, and notifications are not shown.

  1. On the lender’s iPhone, go to Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, turn it on, and set an unlock passcode under Passcode Settings
  2. Open panovi, quickly press the side button (or the Home button) three times, and tap Start at the top right
  3. When you are done, press it three times again, enter the passcode, and tap End at the top left

The footage also stays on the borrowed iPhone (inside panovi and in its photo library). Stitching happens on your own iPhone, so once the transfer is done, ask the owner to delete it. For long recordings, check the borrowed iPhone’s free space first.

How to build the tripod rig. Take any tripod you own, add a dual bracket plate (Amazon), and screw on two MagSafe tripod mounts (Amazon). The iPhones snap on magnetically, so mounting and removing them on site takes a second. Place the two so the lenses meet near the center — the in-app placement check guides the angle.

11 — FAQ

Things you may wonder.

Is my footage sent anywhere?

No. All processing happens on the iPhones. No account, no login, and your footage never touches the internet. It works on fields and mountaintops with no signal.

How many iPhones do I need?

Two for shooting. A third can act as a remote in your hand: start and stop recording, check the seam, see each camera’s battery and free space, and pull videos over (optional). You can also try panovi with a single iPhone first — sample videos are built in, so you can see what a stitched result looks like.

Do the two iPhones have to be the same model?

No. Different models work — the app automatically picks a lens both can use.

Do I need a tripod?

Yes, to hold the two phones side by side. Use one that can carry both; the trick is getting the lenses close together.

What if the tripod gets bumped mid-recording?

The stitch can be split at the bump and recalibrated. If a result looks off, retry options are right on the screen.

How long can I record?

There is no recording limit — as long as battery and storage last. A power-save mode can turn the screen black while recording to stretch the battery. Only the stitch length affects pricing.
