★ How It Works

Tap the star.
That's it.

While recording, tap the star whenever something happens — a goal, a great save, a highlight play. When you stop, YouTube chapter timestamps are in your clipboard. Paste into the description. Done.

Shoot close.
Widen it later.

Wide Later Photo and Wide Later Video shoot with the wide and ultra wide lenses at the same time. If you later think “the play in front of the goal got cut off” or “I wanted the whole field in,” tap “Widen the shot” in the app. You can widen the frame out to what the ultra wide lens saw.

Wide Later Photo

Keep your usual shot.
Remake the wide one anytime.

Your camera roll gets one photo that looks just like a normal photo, at the zoom you shot it.

Later, tap “Widen the shot” in the app to remake it with the frame widened out to the ultra wide edges. The two original shots stay in the app with almost no loss in quality, so you can redo it as many times as you like.

Wide Later Video

Record with two lenses.
Widen and export later.

Both lenses record at the same time, and the two clips (wide and ultra wide) also go straight to your camera roll. Later, use “Widen the shot” to export a video with a wider frame and save it to your camera roll.

The ★ works in Wide Later Video too.

With Wide Later Photo, the moment you shoot, you get a “click” you can feel: a vibration, a sound, and a quick dim of the screen (no sound in Silent mode).

* Wide Later Photo and Wide Later Video only work on iPhones that can shoot with two lenses at the same time.
