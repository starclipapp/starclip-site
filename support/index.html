STARCLIP - Instant Camera & Highlight Marker

Frequently Asked Questions

Basics

The camera preview is black.

Make sure you have granted camera permission in Settings > STARCLIP > Camera. If the issue persists, try restarting the app.

How do I use the highlight feature?

Switch to Video mode, start recording, then tap the star button whenever you want to mark a moment. When you stop recording, the timestamps are automatically copied to your clipboard in YouTube chapter format.

Where are my photos and videos saved?

Your usual photos and videos are saved directly to your device's Photo Library (camera roll). Make sure photo library permission is granted in Settings.

Only for Wide Later Photo and Wide Later Video, the two original shots are also kept inside the app, so you can widen them later. See “Do the originals kept in the app use iPhone storage?” below.

Recording & Playback

Can I take a break without stopping the recording?

Yes (from version 1.1.0). Pause without stopping, and pick up the same video later. Paused time isn't counted in the recording, so your ★ times still match the video length.

Can I zoom in while watching a video?

Yes. On the playback screen, spread two fingers to zoom in, and drag to look around. Double-tap to go back.

Wide Later Photo & Video 1.2.0+

What are Wide Later Photo and Wide Later Video?

They shoot with the wide and ultra wide lenses at the same time. Later, tap “Widen the shot” in the app to widen the frame.

What goes into my camera roll with Wide Later Photo?

Your camera roll gets one photo at the zoom you shot it. The two original shots stay in the app with almost no loss in quality, so you can remake the wide one as many times as you like.

How do I save a Wide Later Video?

Both lenses record at the same time. Later, use “Widen the shot” to export a video with a wider frame and save it to your camera roll. The ★ works in Wide Later Video too.

Where can I find the photos and videos I shot?

Tap the small thumbnail at the bottom left of the camera screen to open the list of photos and videos you shot. Tap “Widen the shot” on a row to open it, or the trash button to delete it.

If you hold your iPhone sideways when you open the list, it lays out wide.

Do the originals kept in the app use iPhone storage?

Yes. The original photos and videos are kept inside the app, so they use your iPhone's storage. When you no longer need them, delete them with the trash button in the list. If you delete the app, they are deleted too.

I can't find Wide Later in the mode switcher.

Wide Later Photo and Wide Later Video only work on iPhones that can shoot with two lenses at the same time. On other iPhones, they don't appear in the mode switcher.

My iPhone vibrates and the screen dims for a moment when I take a Wide Later Photo.

That's the signal that the shot was taken. With Wide Later Photo, the moment you shoot, you get a vibration, a sound, and a quick dim of the screen (no sound in Silent mode).

Privacy

Does STARCLIP collect my data?

No. STARCLIP operates entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
